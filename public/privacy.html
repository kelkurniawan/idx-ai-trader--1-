Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

This template is provided for launch readiness and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before public launch.

Information We Collect

SahamGue may collect account information, authentication identifiers, profile details, subscription and payment status metadata, portfolio and trade journal entries, uploaded chart images, support messages, device data, and usage logs needed to operate and secure the service.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide market analysis tools, maintain accounts, process subscriptions, prevent abuse, improve reliability, respond to support requests, and satisfy legal or security obligations.

Payments

Payments are processed by third-party payment providers. We do not intentionally store full payment card numbers. Payment provider identifiers, invoice IDs, plan status, and transaction metadata may be stored for billing support and reconciliation.

AI And Market Analysis

Chart images, ticker symbols, technical indicators, and related prompts may be sent to AI providers to generate analysis. Do not upload sensitive personal information in chart images or prompts.

Cookies And Authentication

We use cookies and authentication providers to keep users signed in, protect sessions, and prevent unauthorized access. Some cookies are required for the service to work.

Data Sharing

We may share limited information with infrastructure, authentication, analytics, alerting, AI, and payment providers solely as needed to operate the service. We do not sell personal data.

Data Retention

We keep account, billing, and trading records for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security. Users may request account deletion through support.

Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards, including HTTPS, access controls, rate limits, encrypted secrets, and provider-managed authentication. No system can be guaranteed perfectly secure.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact the SahamGue operator at the support address published on the production website.
